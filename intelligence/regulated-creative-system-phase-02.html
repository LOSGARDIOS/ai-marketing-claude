02 — עברית ראשה (Hebrew-First Creative Principles)

All consumer-facing copy is written in natural spoken Israeli Hebrew. English appears in this document for internal context only. The following principles govern Hebrew creative writing for this account.

Principle 1 — Natural Israeli Spoken Hebrew
עברית ישראלית מדוברת — לא עברית שיווקית מתורגמת

כל הטקסט נכתב כאילו מישהו ישראלי אמיתי אומר אותו בקול. לא "לחוויה בלתי נשכחת" — אלא "כי פשוט שווה". לא "המוצר המובחר שלנו" — אלא "זה הטוב ביותר שיש".

Principle 2 — Short. Direct. Israeli.
קצר. ישיר. ישראלי.

ישראלים לא אוהבים פרסומת ארוכה ומסורבלת. כל משפט צריך לעמוד לבד. אם ניתן לגזום מילה — גוזמים. הסגנון: שיחתי, ישיר, לפעמים מתוחכם, לפעמים הומוריסטי.

Principle 3 — Cultural References
מציאות ישראלית — לא אמריקאית

ההפניות הן ישראליות. שישי אחר הצהריים. שוק הכרמל. ביתא ישראל. קהילה. שורשים. אוכל. ריחות. לא "lifestyle". לא "wellness journey". לא "take your life to the next level".

Principle 4 — Humor When Appropriate
הומור ישראלי — כאשר מתאים

הומור ישראלי הוא ציני, מהיר, ועושה קצת כאב. אם הקונספט מתאים לאירוניה — לוקחים אותה עד הסוף. לא חצי הומור, לא מנומס מדי.

Principle 5 — What We Never Say
מה שאסור לכתוב

אסור: טענות בריאות ("מגביר אנרגיה", "משפר מצב רוח", "מחזק מערכת החיסון"), טענות ביצועים, הפניות לאפקטים פסיכואקטיביים, השוואות תזונתיות, שפה שיכולה להתפרש כפרסום סם.

04 — Policy Risk Framework

Every creative concept is assigned a policy risk level. This applies to the channels where the content will be distributed (owned, organic, local portal, OOH) — not hypothetical paid platforms that are categorically unavailable for khat.

LevelDefinitionAction
LOW Content depicts cultural, sensory, origin, or community aspects. No health, therapeutic, or performance claims. No regulatory ambiguity. Compliant with Israeli advertising law as written. Proceed to production. Standard compliance review before publication.
MEDIUM Content contains elements that could be interpreted as implying benefits beyond taste/culture. Wording or imagery is ambiguous under Israeli Consumer Protection Law. Requires deliberate framing. Produce only when strategically justified. Require legal review before publication. Document the compliance rationale.
HIGH Content makes explicit or strongly implied health/performance/stimulant claims. Could attract MOH attention or constitute misleading advertising under Israeli law. Clearly deceptive framing. Do not produce. Do not publish. Flag and remove from pipeline.

Risk Triggers — What Creates MEDIUM or HIGH Risk

Mention of energy, alertness, focus, moodHIGHDirect or implied stimulant / performance claim
"Your body already knows" / "feel the difference"HIGHImplies physiological effect — prohibited health claim

Before/after visual transformation of a personHIGHClassic prohibited comparative advertising format
Social gathering — energy/liveliness in groupMEDIUMLively group setting could imply stimulant effect
Botanical heritage, origin story, harvestLOWCultural and provenance claims — fully permissible
Taste, aroma, texture, sensory experienceLOWSensory claims — permissible for food/botanical products
Family, tradition, memory, communityLOWCultural/emotional framing — permissible

05 — Four-Creative Weekly Pipeline

Each weekly batch is structured across four creative roles. The distribution can shift based on performance data. In weeks without data (Week 1), defaults apply as shown below.

C-01
SAFE WINNER
Low risk + highest commercial potential

The concept most likely to perform well and generate real audience response. Proven format, clear product communication, no policy ambiguity. This is the creative the week is measured by.

C-02
STORY
Animation-based storytelling — emotional connection

A narrative concept that builds brand relationship, not just product awareness. Can be longer (25–45 sec). Measures depth of engagement, not just reach.

C-03
VARIATION
Performance variation of the best existing concept

Week 1: variation of C-01 (different hook, same core idea). Week 3+: variation of the actual top-performing creative from prior weeks. Tests whether the concept is format-dependent or inherently strong.

C-04
EXPERIMENT
Unusual concept — within policy boundaries

A creative territory or format we have not tried. Can be MEDIUM risk if there is a strong strategic reason. Must still be honest, non-deceptive, and within Israeli advertising law. This is where innovation comes from.

Concept

A short, fast-paced 2D comedy sketch. An animated "typical Israeli" character — a bit loud, opinionated, lovable — is at a Friday gathering. They make a series of very Israeli observations about the gathering. The humor is entirely in the character's personality, not in any product claim. At the end, the character pulls out khat — the joke is that this is simply what you bring to a Friday. The humor is cultural, not product-benefit-based.

Critical compliance note: The character's energy and humor must be established before khat appears. The khat must not be presented as the cause of any personality trait or social success shown in the video.

Product Communication
What the viewer understandsKhat is what an Israeli character brings to Friday gatherings — it's normal, social, cultural.
What they do NOT understandWhat khat does physiologically. Any claimed benefit.
When is product revealedSecond 16–18: the khat appears as a punchline item in the character's bag.
Risk to watchCharacter energy before the khat appearance must not create a "before/after" impression. The character is lively throughout — not a transformation.
Misleading impression riskMEDIUM — the cultural "every Israeli knows" framing could imply broader familiarity/normality than actually exists. Monitor and flag if claims emerge in audience response.
