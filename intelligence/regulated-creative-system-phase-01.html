01 — Before Any Creative Work

Critical Compliance Gate

These questions must be answered before any asset enters production. Proceeding without them exposes the client to legal liability and guarantees platform rejection.

🔴 RED GATE — Khat Beverage / Juice / Extract

Israeli law enforcement has arrested individuals for manufacturing and selling khat-derived beverages. The Anti-Drug Authority classifies khat juice and extracts as illegal, regardless of the plant's tolerated status. If the client's product is a khat-infused drink, juice, tincture, or extract, no advertising work can proceed until the product's legal standing is formally confirmed by Israeli legal counsel. This is not a platform policy question — it is a product legality question. A creative system built on an illegal product is a liability, not an asset.

🟡 YELLOW GATE — Khat Leaves (Fresh, for Chewing)

Fresh khat leaves for personal chewing are commercially sold and legally tolerated in Israel. No specific statutory advertising prohibition exists for raw plant material. However: major digital platforms (Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest) universally prohibit khat advertising under their controlled substance and "unsafe substances" policies, even in markets where the product is legally sold. The available advertising territory is significantly narrower than a typical consumer product. Advertising must avoid all health, therapeutic, or performance claims.

🟡 YELLOW GATE — Cannabis (Israel)

Medical cannabis is legal and expanding in Israel. Consumer-facing advertising requires written approval from the Ministry of Health Director General — rarely granted. The IMCA June 2021 clarification permits factual product information on company websites only (name, composition, image, approved leaflet, price, pharmacy availability). Recreational cannabis advertising is prohibited in all forms.

Jurisdiction Required

Final compliance approval for any creative asset requires confirmation of: (1) target country and specific region; (2) exact product form (raw plant vs. processed); (3) licensed status of the business in each target market; (4) intended channel (paid digital, OOH, owned media, organic, print). Do not assume a product is advertising-eligible because it can be sold legally somewhere.

Cannabis — Israel Framework

Medical Cannabis — Legal

~143,900 licensed patients (May 2026). Jan 2024 reform: physicians can prescribe directly without special IMCA license. CBD products under 0.3% THC removed from Dangerous Drugs Ordinance (Feb 2024).

Recreational — Decriminalized

First offense: ~NIS 1,000 civil fine. Not criminal for personal amounts (≤15g). Sale and trafficking remain criminal offenses. No legal advertising pathway.

Advertising — Tightly Restricted

Section 42 of the Dangerous Drugs Regulations (1979): advertising requires MOH Director General written approval + professional literature only. IMCA June 2021 permits factual website-only product info. No consumer-facing paid advertising without prior approval.

Jurisdiction Required Before Final Approval

The following must be provided by the client before any creative receives final compliance approval: target country and state/region for each campaign; exact product form; business license status in each jurisdiction; intended advertising channel(s).

האסיף — The Harvest
Agricultural origin documentary · Highland sourcing story
Green Cinematic Long-form

Core Idea

A documentary-style portrait of khat cultivation — the Ethiopian or Yemenite highlands, the farmers, the harvest, the journey to Israel. The product is never "advertised" — it is witnessed. Viewers understand what they're seeing; there is no deception about the nature of the plant.

Psychological Mechanism

Origin stories confer legitimacy and premium positioning. Showing the labor behind the product builds respect. This is the mechanism that elevated specialty coffee and artisanal olive oil into cultural cachet.

Visual Language

Golden-hour light on hillside farms. Weathered hands sorting leaves. Close-up macro of the fresh green plant. Minimal on-screen text. Cinematic aspect ratio (2.39:1). Natural audio — wind, birds, language.

Tone

Documentary. Quiet pride. Unhurried. No voiceover selling anything.

Product Relationship

The plant is the subject. No claims about its effects. No consumption shown. Truthful representation of agricultural origin.

Platform Fit

Website (hero video), YouTube organic, Instagram Reels organic, PR/press pitching. Ineligible for paid promotion on any major platform.

07 — System Design

Production Strategy

This is not a content calendar. This is a creative learning system. Each week produces assets, each asset generates data, each cycle of data improves the next round. The objective is not simply to produce 4 creatives per week — it is to build a compounding creative intelligence that gets more effective over time.

Weekly Structure

Creative SlotPurposeRisk LevelRelationship to Previous
Creative 01
New Concept		 Introduce a new creative territory or significant departure from existing work Medium Explores new territory. No relationship to existing assets required.
Creative 02
Performance Variation		 Variation of the strongest performing concept from prior weeks — different hook, same core idea Low Builds on proven concept. Isolates one variable (hook, opening image, first line).
Creative 03
Hook Test		 Same underlying value proposition as an existing asset, entirely new first 3 seconds Low Tests whether the concept works with a different entry point.
Creative 04
Experimental		 Higher production risk. New format, new medium, unexpected creative approach. Must remain fully compliant. Medium-High Independent from other slots. The innovation engine of the system.

Creative Learning Loop

Research → Identify Opportunity → Generate Concepts → Compliance Filter → Platform Filter → Score → Produce → Distribute → Collect Data → Identify Winning Elements → Generate Variations → Repeat.

Every four weeks: review which creative territories are generating the strongest engagement, brand perception signals, or business results. Increase allocation toward winning territories. Retire underperforming ones. Introduce one new experimental territory per cycle.

Non-Negotiable Quality Standard

Before any asset is finalized, answer: "If the logo and product were removed, would this still be an interesting piece of content?" If the answer is no, the concept needs revision. Generic stock footage, generic "happy people," meaningless cinematic shots, and obvious AI artifacts are disqualifiers regardless of schedule pressure.
